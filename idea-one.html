Idea One

Clearer routes into Ogier's expertise.

The homepage currently directs visitors through three cards — Legal, Corporate and Fiduciary and Consulting. Both concepts below keep those routes, give them greater presence on the page, and make the destination more useful once a visitor has chosen.

The opportunity. The three routes are named after the way the firm is organised. A client with a fund to establish is rarely certain whether that begins with Legal or with Corporate and Fiduciary, and in most cases the answer is both — which is the strength of the model, and the part the current component does not convey. The concepts that follow either defer that question until we have helped, or answer it on the visitor's behalf.

Concept 1

A prominent entry panel

A single panel lifted over the hero, with a short statement of what the firm does on the left and the three routes stacked on the right. Each route previews the services behind it, and opens in place rather than loading a new page.

  • Raises the choice up the page. The panel overlaps the hero, so all three routes are visible without scrolling.
  • Shows what sits behind each route. A preview of the services within lets visitors choose with confidence.
  • Keeps visitors on the homepage. Selecting a route opens the second level in place, preserving the hero.

Concept 2

Routes led by client need

Visitors select the matter they have in hand. The site responds by showing which parts of the firm are involved — usually more than one — together with the relevant services, the jurisdictions clients most often choose, and a named contact.

  • Presents the firm as clients experience it. Two teams engaged on a single matter is the clearest expression of how Ogier works.
  • Matches the language clients use. Enquiries arrive described as matters, not as departments.
  • Loses nothing. The three routes remain beneath, for visitors who already know where they are going.
