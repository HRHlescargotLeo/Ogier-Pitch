Idea Three

Making the insights library answer the question.

News and insights holds 4,392 deals, insights, guides, briefings and newsletters, filtered by service, sector, location, type, topic, year and author. It is a formidable body of work. This idea places a layer over it that responds to a question directly, and shows the Ogier articles the response is drawn from.

The opportunity. The listing serves a reader who already knows what they are looking for. A reader who arrives with a question — what has changed for fund managers this quarter? — must first translate it into a combination of filters before the archive can help them. That translation is work the site can do on their behalf, and the material to answer with is already published.

The concept

Ask the archive

A question field above the listing. The response is written from Ogier's own published material, every claim numbered to the article it came from, with the jurisdiction switchable so the same question can be asked of a different market.

  • Sources are the point. For a law firm an unattributed answer is a liability; an attributed one puts the archive to work.
  • It returns readers to the articles. The response is a route into the library, not a replacement for it.
  • Older work re-enters circulation. Well-argued pieces from previous quarters resurface wherever they answer a live question.

Beyond the reader

Four things this gives the firm

The value is not only in the answer. A question field over four thousand articles produces information the marketing and knowledge teams do not currently have.

A record of demand

Every question asked is a signal. Subjects that come up repeatedly and are answered thinly show exactly where the next briefing should go.

Subscription at subject level

Sign-up moves from a single list to following a service, a sector or a jurisdiction — better for the reader and better for engagement.

The back catalogue earns again

Articles published two years ago continue to work wherever they answer a current question, rather than sinking down a date-ordered list.

Better visibility in search

Question-shaped pages with attributed answers are the format search engines and AI assistants increasingly surface.

Effort
Significant. Retrieval quality, accuracy of attribution and editorial sign-off all matter.
The real work
A review process: which responses a partner has approved, and how they are retired as the law moves.
Suggested phase
Phase three, and worth piloting internally as a knowledge tool before it faces clients.
