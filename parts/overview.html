What we found

A site with a great deal to say, and not enough ways to say it to the right person.

We spent time across the homepage, the Expertise tree, a sample of service pages and the News and insights archive. The craft is not in question — the copy is direct, the tone is distinctive, the publishing cadence is genuinely impressive. The gap is between all that material and the person trying to find their way to the part of it that concerns them.

Working well

  • A confident, human voice. "Focused on results, inspired by people" and "you don't pay for a learning curve" do more than most firms' whole About pages.
  • Genuine breadth, clearly organised. The Expertise tree is complete and consistent — services, sub-services and sectors all hold together.
  • Publishing few can match. Deals, insights, guides, brochures and newsletters, tagged by service, sector, location, type, topic and author.
  • The joined-up model is real. Legal, Corporate and Fiduciary and Consulting genuinely work on the same structures.

Where the roadmap starts

  • The homepage routes by org chart. Three equally-weighted cards named after internal business lines, sitting below the fold on most laptops.
  • The joined-up model is invisible. The site presents three separate doors; the pitch is that clients walk through two of them at once.
  • The archive needs a guide. Seven filter dimensions across 4,392 items is a research tool, not a discovery tool.
  • Nowhere to ask a question. Search is keyword-only, so a visitor with a question has to already know the vocabulary of the answer.

The concepts

Idea One

Audience routing on the homepage

Three replacements for the Legal / Corporate and Fiduciary / Consulting component — each with a better second screen behind it.

01The shelf. A white plane lifted over the hero, routes stacked with hover previews. UBS-adjacent, quickest to ship.
02Three doors. Full-height panels that open into a browsable menu, with the other routes kept as rails.
03Start with the need. Pick a situation; watch two business lines light up together. The one-firm story, made visual.

Idea Two

An intelligent digital concierge

Three levels of ambition for an AI guide, drawing on the Lexus Europe model — from a contextual helper to a lead-generating journey.

01Ogier Guide. A dock that knows which page you are on and answers in tables, people cards and reading lists.
02Ask Ogier. Answer-first search over the archive, with visible citations and a jurisdiction switch.
03Structuring Companion. Four questions to a jurisdiction shortlist, the services behind it and a named first call.
